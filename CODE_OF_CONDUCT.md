# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and make it easy for them to assume the same about you.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion about the work: the code, the behaviour, the trade-offs.
- Accept that this is a hobby project maintained in spare time. Reports and pull requests may wait; "bump" is fine, entitlement is not.
- Disagree on the technical merits. "This breaks X because Y" is useful; "this is garbage" is not.
- Respect a decision once it is made and explained, even if you would have chosen differently.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.
- Discriminatory or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing threads, or persistently reopening settled decisions to wear people down.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project is represented: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and release notes.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at 34617144+ozaneski13@users.noreply.github.com. Reports are handled discreetly and as promptly as a single maintainer can manage.

The maintainer may edit or delete offending contributions, and may block accounts from the repository. Enforcement is proportionate: a first, mild incident gets a warning; repeated or severe behaviour gets a block.

*Written for this project, in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).*
